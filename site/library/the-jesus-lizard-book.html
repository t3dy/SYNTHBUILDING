The Jesus Lizard Book

Various (band members, Corey Rusk, and contributors) · E:\pdf\music\the jesus lizard book_the jesus lizard.pdf

The Jesus Lizard Book is a band-authorized retrospective (not written by an outside critic but assembled from the band's own and collaborators' testimony) documenting the Chicago-via-Austin noise-rock band the Jesus Lizard: guitarist Duane Denison, bassist David Wm. Sims, drummer Mac McNeilly, and singer David Yow, with an introduction by Corey Rusk, founder of Touch and Go Records, the label that released the band's classic run. Structurally the book proceeds chronologically through the band's discography — Pure (1989 EP), Head (1990), Goat (1991), Liar (1992), Down (1994), and Shot (1996), with Blue (1997) noted separately — interspersed with a performance chronology and contributor biographies, functioning as an album-by-album oral history of the band's formation (Denison's post-Cargo Cult songwriting in Austin, recruiting Yow and Sims from the recently dissolved Scratch Acid, Sims's stint in Rapeman with Steve Albini before the band's actual formation in Chicago) through their major-label-adjacent late-career work. It documents the band's ferocious, improvisationally loose but musically exacting live reputation and the making of each record, mostly in participants' own words rather than as outside critical analysis — closer to an band-history scrapbook/companion than a music-theory or production text. It is written for fans of the band and of the Touch and Go/noise-rock scene, assuming familiarity with or curiosity about the records already. For SYNTHBUILDER this book has no technical relevance — no synthesis, DSP, recording technique, or sound design content — and the band's raw, loud, guitar-driven aesthetic is about as far from chiptune/synth programming as this library gets. Its place is purely as music-history/fandom reading reflecting Ted's personal taste, alongside the 33 1/3 titles.
