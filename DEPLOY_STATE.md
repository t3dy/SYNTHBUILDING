## The base-path gotcha (read before deploying)

Per workspace policy: GitHub Pages serves from a repo subpath
(`t3dy.github.io/SYNTHBUILDING/`, not the root). The generator currently
emits **relative** links (`project/<slug>.html`, `../style.css`, etc.) with
no root-absolute paths — confirmed by grep-equivalent inspection of
`build_site.py`'s templates, no `href="/` or `src="/` anywhere. This should
survive the subpath move without the classic 404-everything failure, but
**re-verify by loading the actual Pages URL after first deploy**, not just
trusting this note.
